# Abuse, takedowns and copyright

*This is a plain-language template, not legal advice.*

## Report abuse

Email **abuse@omnexlab.site** or open an [abuse report issue](../../issues/new?template=abuse-report.md). Include the subdomain, what is wrong and any evidence. For phishing or malware, email rather than posting publicly.

We aim to read reports within 48 hours. Clear phishing and malware reports are handled as fast as we can.

## What we do

- Remove the DNS record (usually the same day for clear abuse).
- Permanently block the owner's GitHub account for serious or repeated violations.
- Where relevant, tell the site's hosting provider.

## Copyright (DMCA and similar)

We provide DNS only and do not host content. If your work is being infringed, you may notify us at the address above with: the work, the infringing URL, your contact details, a statement that you are acting in good faith and that the information is accurate, and your signature. We may remove the DNS record and will also forward the notice to the hosting provider where known. Please also report to the host, since only they can remove the content itself.

## Counter-notices and mistakes

If you think a record was removed in error, email us with the subdomain and an explanation.
