Security notice

Do not use a free shared subdomain for sensitive logins, private data or payments. Use your own domain for those. These subdomains are for portfolios, demos and side projects.

Through Cloudflare default

Instant HTTPS. Visitors connect to Cloudflare, which decrypts HTTPS under our Cloudflare account and re-encrypts it to your host. Fine for public sites.

Direct to my host

DNS only. Visitors connect straight to your host, so HTTPS terminates there and Cloudflare never sees the traffic. Your host issues the certificate, which can take a few minutes.

Questions

How long does it take?

Usually about a minute after you submit, once your host has the name attached.

Why do I add the name to my host first?

If a name points at a host that does not have it attached, anyone else can attach it to their own project and serve their content on your address. So the bot only creates the record once your host has the name, and a daily check removes names whose host no longer has them. You are told on GitHub when that happens.

Why was my request rejected?

The bot replies on your request with the reason. Common ones: your host does not have the name attached yet, the name is taken or reserved, the host is not supported, or your GitHub account is under 30 days old.

Is it safe for logins or payments?

No. Free shared subdomains are for portfolios, demos and side projects. With the default Cloudflare proxy, HTTPS is decrypted at Cloudflare under our account before it reaches your host. Choose Direct mode, or use your own domain, for anything sensitive.

Can I have more than one subdomain?

One per GitHub account for now.

Can I use it for email?

No. Subdomains are for websites only.

Do you host my site?

No. We only provide the DNS name. Your site stays on your own host, and you are responsible for it.

How do I cancel my subdomain?

Use the manage page. Only the owner can cancel. The DNS record is removed within minutes.

What if someone misuses a subdomain?

Report it on our abuse and DMCA page. We review reports within 48 hours and can remove a name at any time.
