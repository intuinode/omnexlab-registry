Questions

How long does it take?

Usually about a minute after you submit. Then add the name in your host's custom domain settings.

Why was my request rejected?

The bot replies on your request with the reason. Common ones: the name is taken or reserved, the host is not supported, or your GitHub account is under 30 days old.

Can I change or remove it later?

Yes. Use the same form and choose Change or Remove. Only the owner can do this.

Can I use it for email?

No. Subdomains are for websites only.

What is not allowed?

Phishing, malware, impersonation, spam and anything illegal. Such names are removed. See the Terms.
