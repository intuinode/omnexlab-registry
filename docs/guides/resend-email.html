Send email with Resend on your subdomain

About 10 minutes, plus a manual review

You can use Resend to send email from your free subdomain through their API. We only publish the DNS records. You need your own Resend account, and your mail goes through Resend, not through us.

Only mail people asked for. Sign-up confirmations, password resets, receipts and replies are fine. Marketing blasts, bought lists, cold outreach and anything unsolicited are not allowed, and remove your subdomain. Read the email rules.

What you need

  • A subdomain you own, for example yourname.omnexlab.site.
  • A Resend account. Check their current free limits and terms.
  • A GitHub account at least 90 days old.

Step by step

  1. Add the domain in Resend. Add mail.yourname.omnexlab.site, with mail. in front. That keeps mail separate from your website.
  2. Keep the Resend page open. It lists DNS records. They come in one of two styles (below).
  3. Send the request. Open the email records form and copy the values. A bot checks them and lists exactly what will be created.
  4. Wait for approval. A person reviews every email request. If you edit the issue, it is checked again.
  5. Verify. When the bot says approved, wait a few minutes and press Verify DNS Records in Resend. It can take longer.
  6. Create an API key. In Resend create a key with sending access only, limited to your domain.

Which values go where

TXT resend._domainkeyDKIM value (starts with p=)
MX send (older style)Sending MX value, for example feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com. We add the SPF record for you.
CNAME send and rsend (newer style)send CNAME target and rsend CNAME target. Leave the MX box empty.
MX for receiving (optional)Receiving MX value, only if you turned on Receiving

Do not add records yourself: we create them, unproxied. We also add a DMARC record that asks receivers to quarantine mail that fails authentication.

Send your first email

Keep the API key on your server, in an environment variable. Never put it in front-end code, a public repository or a GitHub issue.

curl -X POST https://api.resend.com/emails \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $RESEND_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "from": "My App <hello@mail.yourname.omnexlab.site>",
    "to": ["someone@example.com"],
    "subject": "Hello",
    "text": "It works."
  }'

Receiving mail

If you turn on Receiving in Resend and give us the MX value, mail sent to anything@mail.yourname.omnexlab.site goes to your Resend account, and you read it with their dashboard or API. We never see it.

Troubleshooting

  • Not verified after an hour: check that the domain in Resend is exactly mail.yourname.omnexlab.site, then compare each value with your request.
  • Values changed in Resend: edit your issue, or open a new request. It is reviewed again.
  • Stop using email: open the form and choose Remove my email records. This is applied without review, and your website stays.
