What "free" means

  • We intend to keep basic subdomains free, but we can change, limit or end the service at any time, as set out in the Terms.
  • Free does not include support, an uptime promise or a guarantee that a name stays yours. Names can be removed for breaking the rules, without notice.

If we add paid plans

  • The price and what is included will be shown before you pay, and this page and the Terms will be updated.
  • Payments will go through a payment provider, so we never handle your card details.
  • Unless the law says otherwise or we state so in writing, payments are non-refundable. Paying does not excuse breaking the rules, and a plan can still be ended for abuse.
  • Anything that is free today may stay free, but is not guaranteed to.

Sponsorship

  • Sponsorships and donations are voluntary gifts, not purchases. They do not create a contract.
  • They do not buy influence. A sponsor gets no say over who may use the service, no exemption from the Terms, no guaranteed uptime, and no special support.
  • We may decline or return a sponsorship, for example if it comes from a source we do not want to be linked with.
  • We will list a sponsor's name or logo only with their permission, and may remove it at any time.
  • Gifts are normally non-refundable, and are not tax-deductible unless we tell you otherwise.
Sponsorship is not open yet. If you would like to help, or have a question about plans, send us a note on GitHub and we will get back to you.
