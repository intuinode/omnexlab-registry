Vercel

  1. Open your project in Vercel, then Settings → Domains.
  2. Type yourname.omnexlab.site and add it. Vercel will warn that DNS is not configured yet. That is expected.
  3. Go to the request form. In "Where your site lives" enter your project's address, for example myproject.vercel.app.
  4. Press the button, then Submit new issue on GitHub.
  5. Within about a minute the bot replies "Done". Back in Vercel press Refresh on the domain.
"Proxy Detected" warning in Vercel? By default our names go through Cloudflare, which gives you instant HTTPS. Vercel recommends no proxy, so it shows a notice. Your site still works. To remove the notice, choose Direct to my host as the traffic mode when you request, or change it later on the manage page.

GitHub Pages

  1. In your repository open Settings → Pages.
  2. Put yourname.omnexlab.site in Custom domain and save. GitHub says the DNS check failed for now. That is expected.
  3. On the request form enter yourusername.github.io as the address.
  4. Submit the request. When the bot says "Done", return to Settings → Pages. It may take a few minutes to go green.

Other hosts

Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitLab Pages, Render, Fly.io and Firebase addresses are accepted. We cannot check them automatically, so add the name there immediately after requesting it. Until then someone else could claim it.

Traffic modes: which should I pick?

ModeHTTPSWhat it means
Through Cloudflare (default)InstantVisitors connect to Cloudflare, which decrypts HTTPS under our Cloudflare account and re-encrypts it to your host. Good for public sites. Vercel may show a "Proxy Detected" notice.
Direct to my hostFrom your host, can take a few minutesDNS only. Visitors connect straight to your host, so HTTPS terminates there and Cloudflare never sees the traffic. Choose this for anything more sensitive than a public site.
Do not use a free shared subdomain for sensitive logins, private data or payments. Use your own domain for those, whichever mode you pick.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
"Your host does not have the name attached"Add yourname.omnexlab.site in your host first (see above), then submit the form again.
"The name is taken" or "reserved"Pick another name. Names that look like big brands or contain words like login or secure are blocked.
"Account must be at least 30 days old"Use a GitHub account that is older, or try again later.
Site loads but shows the host's 404The name is not attached to the right project at your host. Check the domain settings there.
Padlock or certificate errorWait a few minutes after the bot says "Done". Make sure the name is attached to your host.
My subdomain disappearedIf your host stopped serving the name, we remove it to prevent a takeover and open an issue to tell you. Attach it again and re-request.
I want to move or stop using itUse the manage page.
