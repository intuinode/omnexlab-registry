DMCA and abuse reports

Last updated 24 November 2026 · General information, not legal advice

We do not host the websites on omnexlab.site subdomains. We only provide the DNS name. We review every report and aim to take action within 48 hours, usually sooner. Removing the DNS name stops the address working; the content itself stays with the site's host, so please report it there as well.

Report abuse

Use this for phishing, malware, scams, impersonation, illegal content, harassment, or any breach of our Terms.

Report on GitHub   or email abuse@omnexlab.site.

Please include: the subdomain (name.omnexlab.site), what is wrong, and evidence such as links or screenshots. For urgent or sensitive reports (phishing, malware, threats), email instead of posting publicly. GitHub reports are public.

Copyright infringement (DMCA)

If you are a copyright owner, or authorised to act for one, and believe content reachable through a subdomain infringes your copyright, send a written notice to abuse@omnexlab.site containing:

  1. your name, address, telephone number and email address;
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you say is infringed;
  3. the exact address (URL) of the allegedly infringing material, and the subdomain involved;
  4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
  5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner;
  6. your physical or electronic signature.

Please be accurate. Knowingly making a false claim can make you liable for damages. We may forward your notice, including your contact details, to the site's owner and its host.

Counter-notice

If we removed a subdomain because of a copyright notice and you believe that was a mistake, send a counter-notice to abuse@omnexlab.site with: your name and contact details; the subdomain and what was removed; a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts where you live; and your signature. We may pass it to the person who complained. Because we only provide the DNS name, we can restore it only if the complainant does not take further action and the other terms are met. We are not required to restore it.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe copyright, or repeatedly break our rules, are blocked and lose their subdomains.

DMCA agent and safe harbor

We handle copyright notices in line with the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). We have not yet registered a designated agent with the US Copyright Office, so we cannot yet point to one. Until we do, send notices to abuse@omnexlab.site; we process them in the same way. Because we only provide a DNS name and host no content, the removal we can offer is taking the name down. Nothing on this page is legal advice.

What we do

  • Review within 48 hours: we read each report and decide what to do. Clear phishing and malware are handled first.
  • Remove the name: the DNS record can be removed at any time, and the subdomain or the whole service can be cancelled without notice.
  • Block the owner: for serious or repeated violations.
  • Pass it on: to the site's host, or to the authorities where the law or safety calls for it.
  • Reply: we may tell the reporter what we did, but we are not obliged to share details about a user.

We are not responsible for the content of user sites and do not guarantee that a report leads to removal. Reports that are unclear, unsupported or abusive may be ignored.

Reporting to the site's host

Content is served by the owner's host, not by us. For fastest removal also report to the host: Vercel, GitHub, or the host named in the site's address.
