1. The service

omnexlab.site ("we", "us", "the service") lets people request a free subdomain of omnexlab.site, such as name.omnexlab.site. The service only creates a DNS record that points the name at a web host chosen by you, for example Vercel or GitHub Pages, and, if you ask and we approve, the DNS records an email provider needs (section 16). We do not host, store, copy, monitor or edit any website, file or content. The service is run through a public GitHub repository, and traffic to subdomains passes through Cloudflare.

You need a GitHub account in good standing that follows GitHub's terms, and you must be old enough to enter a binding agreement where you live (at least 16 if you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, at least 13 elsewhere). If you use the service for an organisation, you confirm you may bind it. By requesting a subdomain you accept these terms.

  • Only the GitHub account that requested a subdomain can change or cancel it.

Not for sensitive use, and the Cloudflare proxy. Free shared subdomains are for portfolios, demos and side projects. 13. Automated safety checks

Every day we check each subdomain's hostname against public threat lists: Google Safe Browsing (where configured) and the malware and phishing filters of Cloudflare's and Quad9's public DNS. Only the hostname is checked; we do not look at your content.

  • Severe (for example phishing or malware reported by Google, or blocked by two independent filters): the DNS record is removed at once, and we tell you on GitHub why and how to ask for a review.
  • Mild (for example "unwanted software", or a single filter): you get a warning and 7 days to fix it. If the site is still flagged after that, the name is removed. If it is clean again, the warning is closed.

These checks are best effort. They can be wrong in either direction, and they are not a promise that a site is safe. If you think a flag is a mistake, email abuse@omnexlab.site. We may also remove names for any other reason in section 6.
