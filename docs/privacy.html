Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: we collect very little. Your GitHub username and subdomain request are public. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Cloudflare and GitHub process some data as our providers.

What we collect

  • Your request: your public GitHub username, the subdomain name, the address it points to, and the text of your request issue. These are stored in our public GitHub repository and issue tracker, visible to everyone, and remain in the Git history.
  • Reports and messages: anything you send to our abuse address or post in a report, including contact details in a DMCA notice. We may share a notice with the site owner and its host.
  • Payment data: none. The service is free today. If paid plans or sponsorship are added, payments will be handled by a payment provider and this page will be updated first.

What this website does not do

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and uses no third-party trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, and those providers may log visits (such as IP address and browser type) under their own policies.

Traffic to subdomains

By default, visits to name.omnexlab.site pass through Cloudflare, which decrypts the HTTPS traffic at its edge (under our Cloudflare account), processes it (including IP addresses) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, and re-encrypts it to the site's host. Cloudflare, and the holder of our account, could technically see that traffic. We do not run the sites and do not collect visitor data from them. If the owner chooses Direct mode (DNS only), visitors connect straight to the owner's host and this does not apply. The owner of each site, and its host, are responsible for their own visitors' data.

Do not use free shared subdomains for sensitive logins, private data or payments.

Why we use it

To create, check, change and remove subdomains, to stop abuse and takeovers, to answer reports, and to keep the service running. We do not sell personal data.

Who else sees it

  • GitHub, where the repository, requests and this website live.
  • Cloudflare, for DNS, HTTPS and traffic protection.
  • Mail provider, for messages sent to our abuse address.
  • Authorities or affected parties, where the law requires or where we pass on a report.

How long we keep it

Request issues and Git history are kept for as long as the repository exists, because that is how the registry works. Mail about reports is kept for as long as needed to handle them and to deal with repeat abuse.

Your choices

You can cancel a subdomain on the manage page; the DNS record disappears within minutes. Public Git history and old issues cannot be fully erased, so do not put private information in a request. You can ask us to delete or correct data we hold about you, and to remove your username from issue text where possible, by emailing abuse@omnexlab.site. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, such as access, objection or complaint to a data authority.

Children

The service is not meant for children under 13 (16 in the EEA and UK). We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by changing this page. Questions: abuse@omnexlab.site.
