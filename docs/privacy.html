Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · General information, not legal advice

In short: we collect very little. Your GitHub username and subdomain request are public. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. We will delete what we can on request, within 30 days and usually much faster. A few public traces (old Git history, copies kept by others) cannot be erased, and we say exactly which below.

Who is responsible

The operator of omnexlab.site is the controller of the personal data described here. The operator is an individual who maintains github.com/intuinode. Their name and postal address are not published; they are protected by domain privacy and are disclosed to authorities, and to anyone else, where the law requires it. You can reach the operator, and exercise every right below, at abuse@omnexlab.site. We have not appointed a data protection officer or an EU representative; if the law requires one, we will.

What we collect

  • Your request: your public GitHub username, the subdomain name, the address it points to, the traffic mode you chose, and the text of your request issue. These are stored in our public GitHub repository and issue tracker, visible to everyone.
  • Reports and messages: anything you send to our abuse address or post in a report, including contact details in a DMCA notice. We may share a notice with the site owner and its host.
  • Safety checks: the hostname of each subdomain (not personal data about you) is sent to threat-intelligence services; see "Who else sees it".
  • Payment data: none. The service is free today. If paid plans or sponsorship are added, payments will be handled by a payment provider and this page will be updated first.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service you asked for (creating, checking, changing and removing your subdomain), because you request it.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service and its visitors safe (takeover checks, safety checks, handling reports), because it is in the legitimate interests of the operator, of visitors and of the other users.
  • To meet legal obligations and defend legal claims, where the law requires it.

We do not rely on consent, we do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

This website

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and uses no third-party trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, and those providers may log visits (such as IP address and browser type) under their own policies.

Traffic to subdomains

By default, visits to name.omnexlab.site pass through Cloudflare, which decrypts the HTTPS traffic at its edge (under our Cloudflare account), processes it (including IP addresses) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, and re-encrypts it to the site's host. Cloudflare, and the holder of our account, could technically see that traffic. We do not run the sites and do not collect visitor data from them. If the owner chooses Direct mode (DNS only), visitors connect straight to the owner's host and this does not apply. The owner of each site, and its host, are responsible for their own visitors' data.

Do not use free shared subdomains for sensitive logins, private data or payments.

Who else sees it

  • GitHub, where the repository, requests and this website live.
  • Cloudflare, for DNS, HTTPS and traffic protection, and for its security DNS filter used in safety checks.
  • Google (Safe Browsing) and Quad9, which receive only subdomain hostnames for safety checks.
  • Mail provider, for messages sent to our abuse address.
  • Authorities or affected parties, where the law requires or where we pass on a report.

International transfers

GitHub, Cloudflare and Google process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the mechanisms those providers offer (for example standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Because the repository is public, your username and request are visible worldwide.

How long we keep it

  • Requests and the issue tracker: as long as the repository exists, unless you ask us to delete them (see below).
  • Mail about reports: up to 24 months after the last action, or longer if needed for a legal claim or to deal with repeat abuse.
  • Safety warnings and removal notices: kept in the issue tracker like any other issue.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy, and complain to a data protection authority. You can exercise any of them, free of charge, by emailing abuse@omnexlab.site. We may ask you to prove the request comes from you, for example by writing from, or commenting on an issue with, the GitHub account concerned. We answer without undue delay and within one month.

Deleting your data

If you ask us to delete your data, within 30 days, and usually within days, we will:

  1. remove your subdomain and its DNS record, and delete its entry (which holds your username) from the current registry;
  2. delete the request issues, comments and warnings of yours in our issue tracker that we are able to delete;
  3. delete mail you sent us about your request, unless we must keep it for a legal reason, and tell you if so.

What we cannot fully erase: Git keeps a history, so older versions of the registry that contained your username remain visible in past commits, and copies may exist elsewhere (forks, archives, search engines). In serious cases, such as a risk to your safety, we can rewrite the history to remove those traces, but that is a drastic step that changes every later commit, so we do it only when it is needed. Because of this, do not put private information in a request, and use a GitHub account you are comfortable having linked to the subdomain.

Automated decisions

A bot decides whether a request is valid, and automated checks remove names that a host no longer serves or that threat lists flag as dangerous. You can ask a person to review any such decision by emailing us.

Children

The service is not meant for children under 13 (16 in the EEA and UK). We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by changing this page and its date. Questions and requests: abuse@omnexlab.site. This page is general information and not legal advice.
