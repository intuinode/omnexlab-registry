# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 23 November 2026. This is a plain-language template, not legal advice.*

**What we collect.** Only what you put in your pull request: your public GitHub username, the subdomain name, the target host and an optional description. These are stored in this public repository and are visible to everyone, permanently in the Git history.

**What we do not collect.** No cookies, no analytics and no tracking on this repository. We do not host or see your site's visitors.

**Third parties.** GitHub processes your pull request and account data under its own policy. Cloudflare provides our DNS and may keep DNS query logs under its own policy.

**How we use it.** To create, check and, if needed, remove your subdomain, and to contact you or your host about abuse.

**Your choices.** To remove your entry, delete your file with a pull request; the record disappears from DNS within minutes. Past Git history cannot be fully erased, so do not put anything private in your file.

**Contact.** abuse@omnexlab.site
